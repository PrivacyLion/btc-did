1. Service Description

SignedByMe provides a cryptographic identity layer for autonomous agents. Our service enables:

  • Self-signing cryptographic identity (DID) for agents
  • Zero-knowledge membership proofs via Groth16
  • OIDC-compatible authentication tokens
  • Human-controlled delegation and revocation via NOSTR
  • Bitcoin Lightning Network payment integration

SignedByMe is operated by Privacy Lion LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

2. Subscription and Payment

The SignedByMe service requires a monthly subscription of $21 USD (or equivalent in Bitcoin satoshis) per agent.

  • Payment is made via Bitcoin Lightning Network
  • Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance
  • Revenue is allocated: 60% SignedByMe, 20% Enterprise, 20% Agent
  • Failure to pay results in delegation expiration and loss of authentication capability

3. Human Owner Responsibilities

As a human owner authorizing an agent, you are responsible for:

  • Safeguarding your NOSTR private key (nsec)
  • Defining appropriate scopes and permissions for your agent
  • Monitoring your agent's activity via public NOSTR events
  • Revoking delegation (kind 28251) if your agent is compromised
  • Maintaining accurate email mappings for enterprise enrollment

You retain sole authority to revoke agent access at any time by publishing a kind 28251 revocation event.

4. Agent Usage Rules

Agents using SignedByMe must:

  • Operate within the scopes defined by their human owner's delegation
  • Not attempt to forge, modify, or replay cryptographic proofs
  • Not attempt to access enterprises where they are not enrolled
  • Store cryptographic material (DID, leaf_secret) securely
  • Respect enterprise-defined access boundaries

5. Enterprise Integration

Enterprises integrating SignedByMe agree to:

  • Validate delegation chains via NOSTR before granting access
  • Respect the privacy-preserving nature of zero-knowledge proofs
  • Not attempt to correlate or deanonymize agent identities
  • Maintain NIP-05 verification at their domain
  • Handle OIDC tokens according to standard security practices

Enterprises receive 20% revenue share for each agent authentication to their service.
